Brand & site direction

A railroad of its own — a sibling to FlatBars, not a clone.

Gramark today is a stock Starlight theme with a strong idea already in its brand/ folder: a railroad diagram that forks into alternatives. FlatBars sets the bar — IBM Plex, one confident accent, an explicit light/dark, and a favicon that is the product. This applies the same rigour to Gramark and gives it a hue of its own: emerald, where FlatBars owns violet.

00

The middle m

The name is a portmanteau — grammar + Markdown mark — elided onto one shared letter: gramark. That single m is the hinge where the two halves overlap, so it's the natural place to carry the brand. Ten treatments, rated — all respect the repo's rules (one emerald accent, no second colour, no M↓ Markdown tile).

Note: I also corrected the wordmark — it was rendering Gram+mark = “Grammark” (two m's). Every wordmark on this page now spells Gramark using the recommended treatment below.
