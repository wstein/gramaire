Gramark is a grammar system whose source format is Markdown. This documentation site bundles the core documentation, formal specs, tutorials, and the interactive Gramark Lab experience for grammar authors.

## What you can find here

- Product overview and architecture notes
- Grammar authoring tutorials
- Formal grammar and IR specifications
- The Gramark Lab experience
