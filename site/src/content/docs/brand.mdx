## Logomark

A railroad / syntax-diagram mark: an entry track meets an emerald rule-node
that forks into two alternatives — exactly what Gramaire turns a grammar into.
Theme-aware ink, constant emerald node.

<LogomarkShowcase />

Sizes shown: 96, 48, 32, 16 px, and the white-knockout variant (used only on
filled-emerald tiles).

## Wordmark

The wordmark uses one weight throughout: `Gram` is ink and `aire` is accent.
The old split-**m** Gramark treatment is intentionally not part of Gramaire's
lockup.

**One accent only.** Emerald always reads "valid." `--node` (`#15b879`) is the
logomark's constant fill; the UI/text accent is the AA-contrast `--accent`
(`#0a8f63` light / `#34d399` dark) — never use `--node` for text or controls.
